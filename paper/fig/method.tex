\caption{\textbf{From residual entropy to \name.} The renderer estimates the residual entropy of every character from the text alone (left: a needle sentence colored by $h_i$; the key and the digits are hot). Dividing by glyph area gives the information load, which can be drawn as a map of any page and summarized by entropy uniformity $\U$. Typical COC leaves the high-entropy strings as hot spots. \name enlarges the flagged strings and shrinks prose within the same vision-token budget, which flattens the map (same page; each cell is one vision token, as in \cref{fig:teaser}). Anchors leave the map unchanged and are optional post-processing. Every step is training-free and needs no access to the model.}
