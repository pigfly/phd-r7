\section{Experimental Setup}
\label{sec:setup}

We treat every VLM as a black box and send one stateless request per item at temperature 0 (0.01 for MiniMax), re-sending unparseable outputs up to three times.
The six readers span vendors, scales, and open weights: Claude Sonnet 5.5, Gemini 3.5 Flash-Lite, GPT-6 Luna (minimal reasoning effort), Qwen3-VL-235B-A22B~\cite{bai2025qwen3vl}, Llama 4 Maverick, and MiniMax-M3.
Means and pooled tests use the five readers other than Claude, which was not run on every arm and is reported per reader only.
Cost is the average number of prompt tokens per task as counted by each provider, including the image, the question, and any appended text. MiniMax reports no usage and is left out of cost averages only.
Empty responses and transport errors (fewer than 100 of roughly 35{,}500 calls) are dropped; any other unparseable output counts as wrong.

Verbatim fidelity is measured with RULER needle-in-a-haystack tasks~\cite{hsieh2024ruler}, re-implemented with the official templates and Paul Graham essays as the haystack.
The tasks are \emph{single} (one UUID value), \emph{multi-key} (four keys, one queried, seven-digit values), and \emph{multi-value} (four seven-digit values for one key).
Contexts have 16K characters (\cref{sec:main}), 32K--128K characters split into up to three pages (\cref{sec:length}), or 32K characters at milder budgets (\cref{sec:regime}).
Collateral damage is measured on held-out LongBench~\cite{bai2024longbench} MultiFieldQA-en and HotpotQA documents of at most 40K characters, drawn after the detector was frozen.
We use $N=25$ items per task and reader for RULER at 16K and 32K characters, $N=20$ for the length study, and $N=50$ for LongBench.

All image arms share one renderer (Menlo, 1008-pixel pages) and the budget $B$ of typical COC at $s_0\in\{5,6\}$\,px, about 32 and 21 characters per vision token on our long documents.
Besides typical \textbf{COC}, the arms are as follows.
\textbf{Font search} picks the largest uniform font and size (Menlo, Arial, Arial Narrow) that fits $B$, a training-free stand-in for global configuration search~\cite{cheng2025glyph}.
\textbf{Downsample} renders at 10\,px and shrinks the image to $B$~\cite{wei2025deepseekocr}.
\textbf{Random words} applies our allocation to random ordinary words whose total length matches the flagged strings.
\textbf{Box} uses our layout without enlargement and draws a box around each flagged string.
\textbf{Half} applies our allocation to a random half of the flagged strings.
As post-processing, verbatim anchors (the flagged strings copied into the prompt) are appended to COC and to \name (\textbf{COC+anchor}, \textbf{\name+anchor}), and \textbf{text} gives the plain text.\footnote{We do not compare with FocusVTC, SEER, or LensVLM, which fine-tune the reader, or with AGAR, which needs attention maps; our readers are black-box APIs.}

RULER is scored by recall of the gold values (case-insensitive containment) and LongBench by token-level F1, as in the original benchmarks.
Because recall does not penalize extra answers, we also report a precision-aware F1.
For each reader, task, and setting (a \emph{cell}) we compute the mean paired difference over items with a 95\% bootstrap interval. Pooled effects average over cells, with intervals from a bootstrap over readers and items, and significant cells are counted after Holm correction (\cref{app:stats}).
