\section*{LLM Usage Statement}
Large language models are the \emph{objects of study} in this work: the six vision-language models listed in \cref{sec:setup} and the additional Claude reader of \cref{app:claude} were queried as black-box readers, and all reported numbers are computed by our scripts from their logged outputs.
Every number in the paper is produced programmatically from the experiment logs, and every reference was checked against its primary source.
No LLM was used to generate or label evaluation data beyond the readers' answers being evaluated.
